  • Browser at 100% zoom, window at least 1600 px wide. The Price Updates grid has fifteen columns.
  • Know the cast below. Bluestem, its customers and its reps are all fictional. Never describe the data as a live tenant or the buttons as ERP postings.
Don't press the primary button in any dialog: Confirm & execute new price, Send email, Create case. Nothing leaves the browser, but each shows a toast saying the price was "executed" or the case "created", which over-claims what the demo did. Open the dialog, talk through the preview, then Cancel. The same goes for Accept / Dismiss on Recommendations and Go live / Save settings on Setup. If someone clicks one anyway, refresh the page; every change lives only in the browser.

It's apple and squash harvest at Bluestem. Every grocery chain wants a fall feature, reps are matching competitors one order line at a time, and promo prices quietly outlive their windows. Questions you will get

Is this live D365?

No. The booth build runs on a bundled Bluestem dataset shaped like D365 F&SC pricing: trade-agreement base price, a price (margin) component, line, threshold, quantity and mix-and-match discounts, and auto charges. Setup shows where the D365 connection plugs in; wiring it to live data is the implementation step.

How is margin calculated?

Sales price = (base + margin component) × (1 − discount) + auto charge. Margin = (sales price − cost) ÷ sales price, with cost net of any grower pool share. A line fails when it is at or below the target, 25% by default.

What's the difference between thin and below floor?

Thin means under the margin target. Below floor means the price is under the price group's floor. Inside an approved trade-promotion window that's agreed; once the promotion ends, or with none on file, it's a price leak. The app keeps the two apart everywhere.

What's grower pool share?

On pooled fruit (apples, blueberries) the grower pool carries part of a promotional price, so Bluestem's real margin is measured against a reduced cost. Pooled lines show the margin twice: pooled, and unpooled underneath.

Why days of cover, not weeks of supply?

Produce has a shelf life. Cover is measured on a trailing 28-day run rate and shown against the item's shelf life, red above 60% of the life used. Nine days is fine for a 60-day squash carton and a problem for a 12-day blueberry pint.

Does the agent change prices by itself?

No. Every action is human-in-the-loop. The reprice dialog solves the chosen price component to a target margin or applies a flat % move, optionally held at the floor, and shows the new price before anyone confirms. Contract lines would route through trade-agreement journal approval.

Is the assistant an LLM?

By default it's an offline heuristic that answers from the same aggregations the pages use, so every number it quotes ties out. Setup has an Azure OpenAI transport option for a bring-your-own-key deployment.

Can we use our own thresholds?

Yes. The 25% floor and the 20% foodservice minimum are guardrails on Setup, and the threshold test takes any margin and date window.

If something goes wrong

  • Blank page or "can't connect". The preview server isn't running. Rebuild and serve with the two lines above. Don't fall back to npm run dev; it hangs on OneDrive.
  • Someone confirmed a reprice, accepted a recommendation, or changed a filter you need. Refresh the page. Every change lives only in the browser session.
  • Dates look stale. Move AS_OF in src/lib/config.ts and rebuild; the whole book slides with it. The recorded reel stays anchored to Oct 8, 2026.
  • The hosted site shows swimwear and beach stores. It is still serving the pre-retarget build. Use the local build until the Oct 8 changes are pushed to main.
  • Nothing loads at all. Fall back to the looping reel while you restart.
